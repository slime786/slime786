Information processed

For orders this may include your name, delivery address, email address, order contents, payment status, PayPal identifiers, fulfilment/tracking information and customer-service correspondence. The shop does not need or intend to store your full card or bank details.

Why it is used and the lawful bases

Order and delivery information is used because it is necessary to take steps at your request and perform a sales contract. Some records are kept to meet legal, accounting or tax obligations. Limited information may also be processed for legitimate interests such as fraud prevention, security, payment reconciliation, resolving disputes and responding to customer-service requests, balanced against your privacy rights.

Who receives information

Information is shared only where needed with service providers involved in the transaction or operation of the shop, including PayPal for payment processing, Supabase for database/server infrastructure, Resend for transactional order email when enabled, and delivery providers used to send the parcel. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

International processing

Some technology or payment providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, the provider and seller must use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism or other safeguard required by UK data-protection law.

Retention

Temporary stock reservations and abandoned checkout data are kept only as long as needed for stock control, reconciliation, security and troubleshooting. Routine enquiries should normally be deleted on a shorter schedule once resolved. Paid-order records are retained only for as long as needed for accounting, tax, fraud, dispute or other legal requirements, then personal fields are deleted or anonymised when there is no continuing reason to identify the customer.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, and receive certain information in a portable form. Where processing relies on consent, consent can be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing. Requests can be made using the contact details above; identity may be checked before order-linked information is released or changed.

Complaints

If a privacy concern cannot be resolved directly, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. See ICO complaints guidance.

Automated decisions & cookies

The shop does not use solely automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects. The storefront does not run advertising or analytics cookies of its own; payment providers may use cookies or similar technology when their checkout is opened, under their own notices and settings.
