WEEKLY / TECHNOLOGY

My lens for reading a noisy technology week.

MOHEEN MAHMOOD · LONDON · LIVING NOTE

A technology news feed can tell me what happened. The more useful question is what deserves attention after the headline disappears.

I tend to look for four things: whether AI is becoming genuinely useful infrastructure; whether a business is building a durable advantage rather than a temporary feature; where constraints are appearing in compute, chips, energy or distribution; and whether a new behaviour is becoming normal for users.

I also try to separate velocity from importance. The fastest-moving story is not always the one with the longest consequences. A product launch can dominate a day while an infrastructure change quietly shapes a decade.

This is why the live Weekly Brief on my homepage is deliberately separate from my authored notes. The feed keeps the site current. These notes are where I decide what I actually think is worth remembering.

As I keep learning, this lens will change too. That is part of the project.
