A PERSONAL COLLECTION

I’m not trying to be another giant card shop.

I collect because I like the artwork, nostalgia and the chase. This site is a simple way to move on duplicates, singles and sealed pieces I no longer need, so they can end up in somebody else’s binder, deck or display.

That means stock will be small, a little random, and personal. When something sells, it is gone unless I happen to own another copy.

— Slime
