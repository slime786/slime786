I use simple collector-friendly condition labels and add plain-English notes where a card has visible issues.

Near Mint (NM)

Very clean overall with only minimal handling or tiny imperfections possible.

Lightly Played (LP)

Minor edge wear, light surface marks, or small whitening that does not dominate the card.

Moderately Played (MP)

Noticeable wear such as whitening, scratches, edge wear, or small bends/creases.

Heavily Played (HP)

Significant wear, creasing, scratches, edge damage, or other obvious defects.

Damaged

Major defects such as heavy creases, tears, water damage, peeling, or structural damage.

Sealed items

Sealed listings will describe wrap condition, dents, tears, shelf wear, and whether the item is factory sealed.

Photos and specific listing notes always take priority over the general label.
