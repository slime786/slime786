This site is designed to collect only the information needed to fulfil orders and respond to enquiries.

What may be processed

Name, delivery address, email address, order details, payment status, and any message you send.

Payments

PayPal processes payment information. Slime's Collectables does not need to store your full card or bank details.

Why information is used

To process orders, arrange delivery, answer questions, prevent fraud, and keep basic business records where required.

Data sharing

Information may be shared with payment providers and delivery services only as necessary to complete your order.

Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for. Paid-order records may be retained where needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal obligations. Customer-service messages that are not needed for an order or dispute should be deleted on a shorter schedule. Records should be reviewed periodically and deleted or anonymised when there is no longer a valid reason to keep the personal data.

Your information

You can use the Contact page to ask about personal information held in connection with an order or enquiry. Requests will be handled in line with applicable data-protection requirements.
