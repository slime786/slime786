SHOP POLICY

Shipping & Returns

Slime's Collectables currently ships within the UK only.

Shipping

Orders are normally dispatched within 1–2 working days. Singles are sent in protective sleeves/top-loaders or equivalent rigid protection. Sealed products are boxed appropriately.

Standard tracked shipping: £3.99 for singles-only orders. Sealed-item shipping: £5.49 where any sealed product is included. Free UK shipping: orders of £100 or more.

Higher-value orders may be upgraded to a more secure Royal Mail service at no extra cost where appropriate.

Returns

For online consumer purchases, you generally have 14 days from delivery to tell us you wish to cancel, then a further 14 days to return the item. Returned items should be in the same condition received. Change-of-mind return postage is paid by the buyer unless otherwise agreed.

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially different from the listing, contact us promptly with photos. Where the seller is responsible, reasonable return postage will be covered.

Sealed products

Sealed products must be returned unopened and in the same sealed condition for change-of-mind returns. Any dents, tears, wrap damage or shelf wear will be disclosed in the listing where known.
