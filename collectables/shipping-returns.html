Shipping

Orders are normally dispatched within 1–2 working days. Standard tracked shipping is £3.99 for singles/non-sealed orders, sealed-item shipping is £5.49 where any sealed product is included, and UK shipping is free for orders of £100 or more.

Higher-value orders: where the normal tracked service does not provide enough compensation for the order value, the parcel will be upgraded to a more appropriate covered service at no extra delivery charge to the buyer.

Packing

Singles are sleeved and given rigid protection before being placed in a suitable mailer. Graded cards are protected against surface damage and movement, then boxed. Sealed products are boxed with protective void fill so corners, seals and wrap are not carrying the impact of transit.

Cancellation

Where UK distance-selling cancellation rights apply, you can tell the seller within 14 days of receiving the goods that you wish to cancel, then return the goods within the following 14 days. You may use the model cancellation form, but you do not have to use that exact wording if your decision to cancel is clear.

For a change-of-mind return, the buyer normally pays the direct cost of returning the goods unless otherwise agreed. Goods may be inspected as reasonably necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning. If handling goes beyond what would reasonably be permitted in a shop and reduces value, an appropriate deduction may be made where the law allows it.

Where a cancellation refund is due, it will be made using the original payment method unless agreed otherwise. The refund will include the basic outbound delivery charge where required by law and will be made within the applicable statutory timeframe, which may depend on receiving the goods back or evidence that they were returned.

Faulty, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed goods

Contact the seller promptly if something is wrong. Photos may be requested where useful to understand transit damage or a listing mismatch. Appropriate repair, replacement, price reduction or refund rights depend on the circumstances and applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy limits statutory rights.

Sealed products

Factory sealed describes an intact original seal, not a promise that packaging is cosmetically flawless. Dents, tears, wrap damage and shelf wear should be disclosed in the listing where known. Opening or handling a collectible product beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect it may affect the amount refundable where the law permits a deduction for diminished value.

If stock cannot be located

If a paid item cannot be physically located or safely fulfilled, no substitute will be sent without your agreement. The affected item or order will be cancelled and refunded rather than silently replaced.
