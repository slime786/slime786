# Slime's Collectables — Fulfilment Guide

Working launch process for real orders. This is operational guidance, not customer-facing marketing copy.

## Before listing an item

- Confirm the item is physically present and is genuinely for sale.
- Record the correct game, set/card number, condition or grade, price and stock quantity.
- Photograph the exact single, slab or sealed product.
- For sealed products, record visible dents, tears, wrap damage and shelf wear.
- Keep personal collection items that are not for sale out of the live catalogue.

## Picking an order

1. Open the paid order and match each line to the exact physical item.
2. Re-check condition against the listing/photos before packing.
3. If anything does not match, stop fulfilment and resolve it before dispatch.
4. Never replace a missing card/product with another version without the buyer agreeing.

## Packing

### Singles
- penny sleeve or appropriate card sleeve;
- rigid protection such as a top-loader or semi-rigid holder;
- protect the opening so the card cannot slide out;
- use a rigid/card-backed mailer or suitable parcel packaging.

### Graded cards
- protect the slab surface;
- wrap against knocks and movement;
- use a crush-resistant box with void fill.

### Sealed products
- do not tape directly to the retail product;
- protect corners, seals and shrink-wrap;
- use an appropriately sized outer box;
- fill voids so the item cannot move freely in transit.

### Bundles
Use the protection required by the most fragile/high-value item in the bundle.

## Delivery service

Customer-facing launch charges:
- singles / non-sealed order: £3.99;
- any sealed product: £5.49;
- £100+ order: free delivery to the buyer.

These are customer charges, not a promise to use the cheapest postage product.

For an order whose value exceeds the compensation available on the normal tracked service, upgrade to a delivery service with more appropriate cover. Record the tracking reference against the order/fulfilment record.

Re-check Royal Mail/service pricing before launch and periodically after launch because carrier prices change.

## Dispatch

- Target dispatch: 1–2 working days.
- Verify the delivery address from the paid order before buying postage.
- Keep proof of posting/tracking.
- Mark the order fulfilled only after the parcel is actually handed to the carrier.

## Cancellation before dispatch

If the buyer cancels before dispatch and cancellation is accepted/required:
- do not send the item;
- return stock to an available state where appropriate;
- refund to the original payment method;
- keep the order/audit record rather than deleting it.

## Returns / problems after dispatch

- Follow the public Shipping & Returns policy and applicable consumer rights.
- For damaged/incorrect/misdescribed goods, ask for useful photos where reasonable.
- Do not make the buyer repeatedly retry payment if PayPal already shows a charge.
- Refund via the original payment route where possible.
- Record the reason and outcome.

## Item cannot be located

If physical stock cannot be found after an order:
1. do not substitute another printing/condition/product without explicit agreement;
2. prevent the unavailable item from being sold again;
3. cancel/refund the affected item or order;
4. correct the inventory count before reopening that listing;
5. record what happened so the stock discrepancy can be investigated.

## High-value order check

Before dispatching a high-value order:
- confirm the exact item and condition one more time;
- photograph the packed item if useful for the fulfilment record;
- choose a service whose compensation/handling is appropriate for the value;
- retain tracking/proof of posting.
