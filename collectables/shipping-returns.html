Slime's Collectables currently ships within the UK only.

Shipping

Orders are normally dispatched within 1–2 working days. Singles are sent in protective sleeves/top-loaders or equivalent rigid protection. Sealed products are boxed appropriately.

Standard tracked shipping: £3.99 for singles-only orders. Sealed-item shipping: £5.49 where any sealed product is included. Free UK shipping: orders of £100 or more.

Higher-value orders: where the normal tracked service does not provide enough compensation for the order value, the parcel will be upgraded to a more appropriate covered service at no extra delivery charge to the buyer.

Packing

Singles are sleeved and given rigid protection before being placed in a suitable mailer. Graded cards are protected against surface damage and movement, then boxed. Sealed products are boxed with protective void fill so corners, seals and wrap are not carrying the impact of transit.

Cancellations & returns

Where UK distance-selling cancellation rights apply, you can tell us within 14 days of receiving the goods that you wish to cancel, then return the goods within the following 14 days. Returned items should be handled only as reasonably necessary to inspect them and should come back in the condition received.

For a change-of-mind return, the buyer normally pays the return postage unless otherwise agreed. If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, faulty, or materially different from the listing, contact us promptly with photos so the appropriate refund, replacement, or return can be arranged. This does not limit any statutory rights that apply.

If you cancel before dispatch, the order will be cancelled and any completed payment will be refunded to the original payment method. If the parcel has already been dispatched, the normal returns process applies.

Sealed products

For change-of-mind returns, sealed products should remain unopened and in the same sealed condition where opening the product would go beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect it. Any dents, tears, wrap damage or shelf wear will be disclosed in the listing where known.

If stock cannot be located

If a paid item cannot be physically located or safely fulfilled, no substitute will be sent without your agreement. The affected order or item will be cancelled and refunded rather than silently replaced with something else.
